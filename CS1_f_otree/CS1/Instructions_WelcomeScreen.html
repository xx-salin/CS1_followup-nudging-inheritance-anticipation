Welcome!

Thanks for participating in our study.

This is a study about spending and savings choices. You will be faced with a scenario, where you receive extra income. You will then be asked to assess how this will change your spending and savings decisions. Note that this study is all about understanding your personal views on spending and savings — there are no right or wrong answers. As part of this study, you will be asked to make hypothetical savings and consumption choices, and state your beliefs and views about financial markets, the economy, and your socio-economic environment. We will also ask a few additional questions.

You will earn the fee announced on Prolific conditional on passing attention checks. It is thus important that you read the explanations carefully and focus exclusively on the study until you are finished.
